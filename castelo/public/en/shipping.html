Delivery area

We only ship within Portugal. For deliveries to the islands (Madeira, Azores) please ask us first.

Delivery of small items

Small items such as tableware, toilet chemicals, chargers etc. are shipped quickly and reliably by parcel service.

Delivery of large items

Bulky or heavy products such as awnings, load carriers, large batteries etc. are shipped by freight carrier. Once the goods have been dispatched, the carrier will phone you within 3 working days to arrange a delivery date. Delivery is to the kerb. Freight costs are quoted on request – in the online shop you can order freight items with Click & Collect or contact us for a delivery quote.

Click & Collect

Orders can be collected from us. Items in stock are usually ready for collection within 30–60 minutes. That saves time and money! Simply choose “Click & Collect” at checkout. Our opening hours.
