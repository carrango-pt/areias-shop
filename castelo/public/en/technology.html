Solar

Solar installation

A solar system is one of the most effective ways to become more independent. When choosing one, we take into account the size of your vehicle and the power needs of your devices, and we only use high-quality components.

  • Advice and power budget calculation
  • Solar panels, MPPT charge controllers, wiring
  • Clean, properly sealed roof entry

That way you can stay anywhere without worrying about power – and save on campsite fees.

Matching parts in our shop: Solar & power

Battery

Battery installation

We check your energy needs and recommend the right solution – AGM, lithium (LiFePO4) or another option. A proper installation protects your devices and makes sure you always have enough energy on the road.

  • Conversion to lithium incl. matching charger and DC-DC booster
  • Battery monitor and management
  • Inverters for 230 V
