FAQ

Good to know

What work do you do?

Motorhome repairs, caravan servicing, solar systems, battery installation (AGM and lithium), electrics and custom conversions. Just ask – we will find a solution.

How do I book an appointment?

By phone, WhatsApp or through our contact form. For inspections and larger repairs we recommend booking ahead so you do not have to wait.

Do you also help holidaymakers who break down?

Yes. Ideally send us a WhatsApp message with a short description and a photo – we will quickly tell you when we can help.

Can I get spare parts and accessories from you?

Yes, in our camping shop at the workshop and online. If something is not in stock, we will order it for you.
